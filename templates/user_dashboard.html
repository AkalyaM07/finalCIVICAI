How CivicAI works

Your complaint is analyzed by CivicAI to identify the issue category, determine its priority, calculate the appropriate SLA, and suggest the responsible department. After submission, the complaint is handled through the administrative workflow. Once an administrator verifies the complaint, assigns it to a department, and updates its status, those changes will be reflected in your dashboard.
